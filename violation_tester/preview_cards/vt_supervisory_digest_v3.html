Understanding this report — how each status is decided
Data fixes required — the record has missing or conflicting dates, or two tasks depend on each other in a loop, so a status can't be calculated until it's corrected.
Immediate action required — the projected finish is already past the due date, or slips beyond the allowed buffer for its priority.
Dependencies awaiting resolution — a task this one depends on hasn't finished yet, so it can't start on schedule.
Needs attention — the projected finish slips into the allowed buffer, or a resource conflict was detected — not late yet, but worth watching.
Stable operations — projected to finish on or before the due date, with no conflicts or unresolved dependencies.
